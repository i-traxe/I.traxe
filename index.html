How It Works

What happens when
you press the button.

A women safety device for night travel and cab rides that works in 3 steps. No app, no unlocking, no internet.

1

Press the SOS button

The button is hidden on the device, so you can press it without taking out your phone. No app, no unlocking. You can do it from your pocket.

2

The device finds your location

Outdoors, the GPS module gives your exact spot. Indoors, where GPS is weak, it falls back to the nearest cell tower, so a location still goes out.

3

Your contact gets a text

An SMS with a Google Maps link goes to the trusted number you set up. It's a normal text, so no internet is needed and any phone can open it, even a basic keypad phone.
